Research Focus:
Bridget Gerovac is part of the UF/IFAS Extension ecosystem focused on environmental outreach, community engagement, water-related education, and applied natural resource programming. Her role centers on implementation, education, stakeholder coordination, and applied environmental systems, bridging scientific institutions, local communities, environmental agencies, and conservation initiatives.

Major focus areas include:

1. Environmental Stewardship: helping communities understand how local actions affect environmental systems, especially water quality and ecosystem health.

2. Public Adoption of Conservation Practices: getting individuals and organizations to adopt evidence-based environmental practices through behavioral communication, incentives, education systems, and trust-building.

3. Stakeholder Coordination: working with local governments, nonprofits, schools, landowners, and community groups to coordinate across multiple stakeholders with differing incentives and varying levels of scientific understanding.

Her Water Institute affiliation connects through water resource education, watershed stewardship, public-facing conservation initiatives, and environmental outreach connected to Florida water systems. In Florida, these issues commonly include water conservation, nutrient pollution, stormwater management, aquifer protection, and sustainable land-use practices.

Her methodological areas include program evaluation, survey research, behavioral outreach, environmental communication, and community engagement analytics.

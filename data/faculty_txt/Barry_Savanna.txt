Research Focus:
Savanna Barry is a Florida Sea Grant Regional Specialized Extension Agent whose work focuses on coastal habitat restoration and resilience along Florida's Gulf Coast, with concentrated effort in the Nature Coast and Cedar Key region. Her applied research spans living shorelines as nature-based alternatives to hardened infrastructure, oyster reef restoration, seagrass (Thalassia testudinum) ecology and blue carbon, and citizen-science monitoring of horseshoe crab populations. She works at the interface of coastal science, waterfront landowner decision-making, and shoreline management policy — translating research into guidance for homeowners, local governments, and restoration practitioners choosing between hardened and nature-based shoreline solutions.

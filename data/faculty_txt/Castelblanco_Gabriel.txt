Research Focus:
Gabriel Castelblanco's work sits at the intersection of construction management, sustainability, water systems, infrastructure resilience, and environmental performance in the built environment. His research is highly interdisciplinary and bridges engineering, sustainability science, construction operations, infrastructure systems, and environmental analytics.

His major focus areas include:

1. Sustainability in the Built Environment: understanding how construction systems, infrastructure processes, and urban development can be redesigned for environmental sustainability, resilience, and long-term resource efficiency. This includes reducing environmental impacts, improving resource management, and integrating sustainability metrics into infrastructure planning.

2. Water & Infrastructure Systems: water-sensitive infrastructure, resilient urban systems, stormwater management integration, and sustainable construction practices affecting water systems. This area increasingly overlaps with climate adaptation, resilient cities, and sustainable urban development.

3. Construction Analytics & Systems Thinking: infrastructure performance optimization, lifecycle modeling, sustainability metrics, and decision-support systems for construction and urban infrastructure. This positions him within a growing movement toward data-informed infrastructure management, smart cities, and sustainable systems engineering.

His methodological areas include lifecycle assessment (LCA), sustainability assessment frameworks, infrastructure analytics, simulation/modeling, environmental impact analysis, systems optimization, resilience evaluation, decision-support systems, multi-criteria evaluation, and infrastructure performance analysis.

His Water Institute affiliation connects through modern water challenges that intersect with urban infrastructure, stormwater systems, sustainable development, and resilience planning. Construction and infrastructure decisions directly impact water quality, runoff, flooding, and watershed health.
